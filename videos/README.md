# Drone videos

Drop a drone clip here and it shows on the progress page ("From the air") on the next weekday refresh (5:30pm Central): near the top the day it was shot and the day after, then below the jobsite photos once it is older. Until a clip is here, the page shows no video section at all.

**Best clip:** 30 to 60 seconds, MP4 or MOV, straight from the drone is fine. The refresh shrinks it to a phone-friendly size, makes a still frame for the cover, and replaces the original. It plays muted and on a loop when someone scrolls to it, with a play button and full screen.

**How, from a phone or computer:** open this folder on github.com, tap **Add file → Upload files**, pick the clip and tap **Commit changes**. GitHub's upload page takes files up to 25 MB; a bigger clip can be sent in the project chat instead and it will be shrunk and added for you.

**Dates and captions:** the drone's recording date is used. To set it yourself, start the file name with the date, and any words after it become the caption: `2026-11-05 framing on Lots 11-14.mp4`. Newest clip shows first; with more than one, the others appear as a row to pick from.

**Longer flyovers (several minutes):** upload them to YouTube as Unlisted, then add a line to `videos/youtube.txt` with the date, a caption and the link:

```
2026-11-05 Full site flyover | https://youtu.be/XXXXXXXXXXX
```
